NewsNative

A native iOS reader for NewsBlur

NewsNative is an independent iOS app for reading your feeds from a NewsBlur-compatible server, either newsblur.com or a self-hosted installation, with native iOS styling and support for the iPhone Duo.

It is made by Mike Waters and is not affiliated with, endorsed, or supported by NewsBlur, Inc. or Samuel Clay. NewsNative is built from the open-source NewsBlur iOS app and is released under the MIT License.

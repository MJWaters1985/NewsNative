NewsNative

Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

NewsNative is an independent iOS app made by Mike Waters. It is a reader for NewsBlur-compatible servers: you sign in to a server you choose, such as newsblur.com or a self-hosted NewsBlur installation, and NewsNative shows your feeds and stories from that server.

What NewsNative collects

Nothing. The developer of NewsNative does not run a server for the app and does not receive any data from it. NewsNative contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting libraries.

Data sent to the server you choose

To work, NewsNative sends information to the server address you enter when you sign in. This includes:

  • your username or email address and password, to sign in;
  • your feeds, folders, read and saved stories, story tags and preferences.

That server's operator, not NewsNative, decides how this information is stored, used and shared, and may pass some of it to their own service providers. Their privacy policy applies. For newsblur.com, see the NewsBlur Privacy Policy.

Content from other websites

Stories can include images, video, audio and links hosted by publishers and other websites. When NewsNative shows that content or opens an original article, your device connects to those websites directly, and their privacy policies apply.

Data stored on your device

NewsNative keeps your sign-in session, preferences, downloaded stories, images and other caches on your device so it can work quickly and offline. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device.

Apple services

  • If you export your preferences, the file is saved where you choose, such as iCloud Drive.
  • If you share diagnostics with app developers in iOS Settings or TestFlight, Apple may provide crash reports to the developer. These do not include your account or reading data.

Children

NewsNative is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about the app or this policy: open an issue on GitHub. Questions about your account or data on a server go to that server's operator.
